# Short-form ad structure (9:16, 15–30s)

## Default 15s skeleton
| time | beat | job |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | **Hook** | Stop the scroll. Movement + bold text on frame 1 (no fade-in from black). |
| 2–5s | **Problem / tension** | The pain, objection, or "before". |
| 5–9s | **Reveal** | Product name + what it does, shown not told. |
| 9–12s | **Proof** | Numbers, reviews, demo, before/after. Only real claims. |
| 12–15s | **CTA** | One action, on screen ≥2s, button + where ("link in bio"). |

30s: double the reveal/proof beats (feature 1, feature 2, testimonial), keep the hook ≤2s.

## Hook formulas (write 3, test them as variants)
- **POV:** "POV: you finally ___"
- **Call-out:** "If you ___, stop scrolling."
- **Negative:** "Stop ___ until you see this."
- **Curiosity gap:** "Nobody tells you this about ___"
- **Contrarian:** "___ is a waste of money. Here's why."
- **Result first:** show the end result, then "here's how".
- **Number:** "3 reasons ___"
- **Question:** "Would you ___ for $__?"
- **Objection flip:** "Scared of ___? Same. Then I tried ___"
- **Us vs them:** "Them: ___ / Us: ___"

## Pacing & legibility
- Change something every 1–2s (new line, cut, zoom, pop). Static frames get swiped away.
- On-screen text: ≤7 words per beat, 70–130px, heavy weight, high contrast, shadow over photos.
- Read time ≈ 0.25s/word + 0.5s. Karaoke captions: 0.16–0.22s per word.
- Safe zone 1080×1920: keep text out of the bottom ~380px and right ~140px; top 120px has the progress bar.
- First frame must already show text (it becomes the thumbnail).
- Sound-off by default: the story must work from on-screen text alone.

## UGC feel (if asked for "UGC style")
- First person, casual lowercase, emoji, "ok so…", "not me ___", reaction faces.
- Phone-native look: captions centered low, slight handheld `float`, quick `whip` cuts.
- Testimonial card: avatar circle, name, ★★★★★, quote in quotes.
